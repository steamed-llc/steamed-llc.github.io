## Fall 2026, NSF Great Plains I-Corps

Two undergraduate students, [Chitrashen Sah](/team/#sah) and [Md Aftabul Islam](/team/#md), from the Department of Computer Science ([CS]), the University of South Dakota ([USD]), conducted 21 customer discovery interviews during their NSF Great Plains I-Corps [Hub]'s training in fall 2026. Identified in these interviews were [NuSim]'s simulation engines in nuclear engineering, [OpenMC] and in biomedical physics, [GAMOS], [TOPAS]-[nBio], [GATE], alongside of its engines for nuclear and particle physics, [Geant4], [Fluka] and [MCNP].

## Fall 2026, Tech2Launch Support

STEAMEd™ received [Tech2Launch] Business Support from [USD] to cover [BBCetc]'s consulting fee in its application to [NIH]'s [STTR] grant, as well as fees for branding (domain names) and marketing ([YouTube]).

## Summer 2026, ALPhA Lab Immersions

Funded by the Advanced Laboratory Physics Association ([ALPhA]), Prof. Jing [Liu] (Founder of STEAMEd™) and his graduate student hosted two hands-on ALPhA [Immersion] workshops. They guided six physics faculty members from universities nationwide through deploying [BEARD] modular detector hardware and [NuSim] cloud radiation simulations in undergraduate instructional laboratories.

## Summer 2025, BEARD in Neutrino Day

The initial version of [BEARD] was developed through two summer internship programs in 2024 and 2025. It was used to demonstrate the detection of invisible particles to thousands of visitors in [Neutrino Day] 2025. Kids got excited by the buzz emitted from [BEARD] when a radiation source got closer.

## Summer 2024, ALPhA Lab Immersion

Funded by the Advanced Laboratory Physics Association ([ALPhA]), Prof. [Liu] hosted a virtual ALPhA [Immersion] workshop for physics faculty members from universities nationwide on [Geant4] simulation. [NuSim] was used for the first time to shrink month-long Geant4 learning time to a few hours.

## Spring 2024, SD Biotech FAST Launch

Supported by the South dakota [Biotech]'s [FAST Launch] program, Prof. [Liu] and hist team extended the customer discovery interviews to 44 cases and drafted the initial version of [NuSim]'s business plan.

## Summer 2023, NSF Great Plains I-Corps

Supported by the [NSF] Great Plains I-Corps [Hub], Prof. [Liu] and his team conducted 20 customer discovery interviews across academic, educational, and industrial sectors, validating market needs and refining the commercialization roadmap for [NuSim].

## Spring 2023, Giant Vision Competition

Prof. Jing [Liu] and his team earned the **4th Place** in the South Dakota Governor's [Giant Vision] Business Competition for the commercial concept behind [NuSim], recognizing its potential to lower technical barriers in radiation simulation and STEM education.
