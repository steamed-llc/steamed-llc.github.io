Key commercialization, workshop, and competition milestones from **STEAM Education Supply, LLC** (STEAMEd™).

## July 2026 — ALPhA Laboratory Immersions on BEARD & NuSim
Funded by the **Advanced Laboratory Physics Association (ALPhA)**, Prof. Jing Liu (Founder of STEAMEd™) and his graduate student hosted two hands-on ALPhA Immersion workshops. They guided six physics faculty members from universities nationwide through deploying **BEARD** modular detector hardware and **NuSim** cloud radiation simulations in undergraduate instructional laboratories.

## August 2023 — NSF Great Plains I-Corps Customer Discovery
Supported by the **NSF Great Plains I-Corps Hub**, Prof. Jing Liu and the STEAMEd™ team conducted more than 40 customer discovery interviews across academic, educational, and industrial sectors, validating market needs and refining the commercialization roadmap for **NuSim**.

## February 2023 — South Dakota Governor's Giant Vision Competition
Prof. Jing Liu and his founding team earned **4th Place** in the **South Dakota Governor's Giant Vision Business Competition** for the commercial concept behind **NuSim**, recognizing its potential to lower technical barriers in radiation simulation and STEM education.
