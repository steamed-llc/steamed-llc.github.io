## 2. Accepted Payment & Ordering Methods
To streamline onboarding for academic and government buyers, we support standard institutional purchasing workflows:

* **Institutional Purchase Orders (POs):** Accepted from accredited colleges, universities, K–12 school districts, and government laboratories (standard **Net 30** terms upon PO verification).
* **Procurement Cards (P-Cards) & Credit Cards:** Secure invoicing via Stripe for rapid departmental orders.
* **ACH / Wire Transfer:** Available for domestic institutional invoices and custom development milestones.
* **Federal Subawards & STTR/SBIR Collaborations:** Ready to partner on NSF, DOE, NIH, and NASA proposals requiring specialized radiation simulation, detector instrumentation, or interactive 3D visualization.
