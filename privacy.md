*Last Updated: November 2026*

**STEAM Education Supply, LLC** ("STEAMEd™," "we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information across `steamed.supply`, `nusim.dev`, `2in.space`, and our related hardware and educational services (collectively, the "Services").

## 1. Information We Collect
* **Information You Provide Directly:** When you request a quote, submit a Purchase Order (PO), subscribe to updates, or contact us by email, we collect your name, institutional or company affiliation, email address, shipping/billing address, and order details.
* **Platform Account & Project Data:** When you create an account or use **NuSim** or **Twin Space**, we store your login credentials, workspace settings, simulation configurations, and uploaded 3D assets or models necessary to provide the service.
* **Payment Information:** Online payments are processed securely through third-party payment processors (such as **Stripe**). We do not store full credit card numbers on our servers.
* **Technical & Usage Logs:** Our web servers and cloud infrastructure automatically record standard diagnostic metadata—such as IP address, browser type, referring pages, and timestamps—to maintain security, prevent abuse, and monitor system performance.

## 2. How We Use Information
We use collected information strictly to:
* Provide, maintain, and improve our websites, cloud simulation platforms, and 3D digital twin tools.
* Process orders, fulfill hardware shipments, issue institutional quotes/invoices, and provide technical support.
* Communicate important service updates, security notices, or responses to your inquiries.
* Protect the integrity and security of our infrastructure against unauthorized access.

**We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or uploaded research/project data to third parties.**

## 3. Educational & Student Privacy
Our educational tools are designed to minimize student data collection. When deployed in K–16 classrooms or university courses, instructors and institutions retain control over student rosters and coursework. We do not use student account data or classroom simulation files for behavioral advertising or third-party marketing.

## 4. Cookies & Analytics
* **Essential Storage:** Our web applications use essential cookies or local browser storage strictly to maintain active login sessions, security tokens, and user interface preferences.
* **Static Corporate Site:** `steamed.supply` is designed to operate without invasive third-party advertising trackers. If we use lightweight, privacy-respecting web analytics, data is aggregated and used solely to understand site traffic and improve documentation.

## 5. Third-Party Service Providers
We rely on trusted infrastructure and service providers to operate our business, including cloud hosting providers, payment processors (such as Stripe), and shipping carriers. These providers process data only on our behalf and are bound by contractual confidentiality and security obligations.

## 6. Data Retention & Security
We retain account, order, and institutional procurement records only for as long as necessary to provide the Services, satisfy accounting or tax obligations, or comply with applicable laws. We apply industry-standard administrative and technical safeguards, including encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections and access controls, to protect data against unauthorized access or disclosure.

## 7. Your Choices & Data Rights
You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal account information by contacting us directly. Note that we may retain certain transaction records as required by tax, accounting, or federal grant/procurement compliance rules.

## 8. External Links
Our websites may link to external repositories, documentation portals, or video channels (such as YouTube, or LinkedIn). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites and encourage you to review their respective policies.

## 9. Changes & Contact
We may update this Privacy Policy periodically by posting a revised version on this page with an updated effective date. For privacy inquiries or data deletion requests, please contact us at [contact@steamed.llc](mailto:contact@steamed.llc).
