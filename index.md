STEAM Education Supply, LLC. is a South Dakota Startup that provides educational resources and materials for teachers, students, professionals and hobbyists. Our mission is to support STEAM (Science, Technology, Engineering, Arts, and Mathematics) education by offering high-quality products that inspire creativity and innovation in learning.

- [BEARD] - Breadboard-based Electronics Assemblies for Radiation Detection
- [beam-on] - Cloud-based Radiation Simulation Platform
